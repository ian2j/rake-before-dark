Rake Before Dark

Rake New England lawns clean before the sun goes down.

How to play
  • Drag across the lawn to rake. Leaves that settle on the blue tarp go in the bag.
  • Dump a big pile on the tarp at once for a ×2, ×3 or ×5 multiplier.
  • Clear the lawn before civil dusk. Daylight you have left scores a bonus.
  • Rake over acorns for power-ups: T big tarp, R wide rake, S sun stop. Pinecones are sticky with pitch and shrink your rake.
  • Things are hidden under the leaves. Each one you find goes on the shelf in your shed.
  • Each town is a five-day week, and every day gets harder. Earn a star to unlock the next day.
  • Every leaf you bag goes to the shed, where you can spend leaves on upgrades.
